Piedmont Countryside

‹ ›
  • Visited April 2026
  • Langhe & Monferrato, Piedmont, Italy
Year Founded Ancient
Population 4.3M
Average Temp 13°C

The Piedmont countryside, encompassing the celebrated hills of Langhe, Roero, and Monferrato, is one of Italy's most remarkable and romantic landscapes. Recognized as a UNESCO World Heritage Site since 2014 under the designation "Piedmont Wine Country," the region's gently undulating hills are blanketed with vineyards, ancient hilltop villages, and farmhouses surrounded by hazelnut orchards. The Langhe area, centered around the town of Alba, produces some of the world's most prestigious wines: Barolo, often called the "King of Italian Wines," and Barbaresco, as well as Dolcetto, Barbera d'Asti, and the sparkling Moscato d'Asti.

Alba, the region's gastronomic capital, hosts the renowned International White Truffle Fair each October, drawing food lovers from across the globe. The surrounding villages, La Morra, Barolo, Castiglione Falletto, and Neive, each offer panoramic viewpoints, family-run wineries, and exceptional local cuisine rooted in the rich agricultural traditions of Piedmont. The culinary heritage of the region, including tajarin (thin egg pasta), braised beef with Barolo, bagna cauda, and hazelnut-based desserts, reflects centuries of refined rural culture. A visit to the Piedmont countryside is an immersion into one of Europe's most authentic and flavorful landscapes.
