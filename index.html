A Glimpse of Torino

For my final project, I will take you on a visual tour through Torino and the surrounding Piedmont region. Through original video, curated photographs, and a focused selection of meaningful locations, this project captures the atmosphere, architecture, and everyday rhythm of the city. It’s designed as a lightweight, visually driven website that blends travel‑guide clarity with the feel of a personal travel journal.

At the Heart of Northern Italy

Turin (Torino) is a historic city in northern Italy, framed by the Alps and known for its elegant Baroque architecture, grand boulevards, and refined café culture. Once the first capital of unified Italy, it blends royal heritage with a modern, understated charm. Walking through its arcades, piazzas, and riverfront streets reveals a city shaped by the House of Savoy, industrial innovation, and a deep artistic tradition.



Beyond the city, the surrounding Piedmont region opens into rolling hills, vineyards, and medieval towns that feel worlds away from Turin’s urban geometry. This landscape, home to Barolo, La Morra, Alba, and countless small villages, is defined by wine culture, slow rhythms, and panoramic views that shift with the light. Together, Turin and Piedmont form a rich visual and cultural corridor that offers both architectural grandeur and quiet rural beauty.
Let's explore it together!
